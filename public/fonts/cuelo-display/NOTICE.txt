Changes from the official GmarketSansTTF download (GmarketSansTTFMedium.ttf, GmarketSansTTFBold.ttf):
- name table: family renamed to "CUELO Display"; only Windows English records kept.
- split into unicode-range subsets (the same ranges as Wanted Sans' web split) and
  compressed to WOFF2 so a page downloads only the characters it shows.
- no glyph outlines were changed.

OFL.txt is the license text embedded in the official font files; the official
download ships no separate license file.
